# TWM Bible 1.3.0 — Study Desk and Share

This release adds two new features: a **Study Desk** for sermon preparation and study notes, and **Share** for making a picture of your favorite verses.

## Study Desk

Open it from the new **Study Desk** card on Home (under Library) or from the **STUDY DESK** button in the top bar (Sermon prep / Notes dropdown).

- **Sermon prep** — a block editor: paragraphs, headings, bulleted and numbered lists, quotes, images, and numbered slide markers. Each block has its own font, size, and alignment.
- Inline **bold**, *italic*, and underline (select text, press B / I / U or Ctrl+B / Ctrl+I / Ctrl+U).
- Undo and redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y / Ctrl+Shift+Z), up to 200 steps.
- Autosave while you type and when the window closes. Keep as many sermons as you like.
- Export a sermon as a web page (.html, images included) or Markdown (.md).
- **Scripture side panel** — look up a passage (`John 3:16-18`, `Ps 23`, `Rom 5:8; 6:23`) or search for a word, check verses, and **INSERT INTO SERMON** as a quote with its reference, e.g. `John 3:16-18 (KJV)`.
- **Notes** — titled study notes, saved automatically.
- Scrolling — the mouse wheel scrolls whatever is under the pointer; press Esc to leave a field, then Up/Down, PageUp/PageDown, Home, and End move through the page.

## Share

1. Hold a word in a verse, then press **SHARE**.
2. Check the verses you want on the card.
3. Pick a background color from the swatches or type a hex color.
4. **Copy picture**, **Save PNG**, or **Copy verse text**.

The Windows installer is unsigned (SmartScreen: More info → Run anyway). The macOS app is not notarized (first launch: right-click → Open). Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Your notes, highlights, and settings from 1.2.0 carry over unchanged.

## Known limits

- In Study Desk, arrow keys do not scroll the side lists (use the mouse wheel).
- Pressing Enter while the edited block is scrolled off-screen does not add a line.
- The STUDY DESK nav button is not highlighted while its dropdown is open from other pages.
